# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We commit to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Respectful and inclusive language
- Constructive feedback
- Empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and issues that violate this Code of Conduct.
