Basin Area Estimator

Daily mean for an evenly sampled hourly series:

\[ \bar{x}_{d}=\frac{1}{N_{d}}\sum_{h=1}^{N_{d}}x_{d,h} \]

Area approximation used in this basin estimator:

\[ A\approx |\Delta lat|\cdot 111.32\cdot |\Delta lon|\cdot 111.32\cdot \cos(\varphi_{mean}) \]
